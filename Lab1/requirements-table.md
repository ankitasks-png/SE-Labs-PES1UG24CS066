# Requirements Table

| ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow patients to grant time-bounded (e.g., 24-hour) access permissions for specific diagnostic records to verified clinic doctors. | High | Pass: Clinic access is automatically revoked upon the specified expiry timestamp. Fail: Clinic accesses records after consent expiration. | Allows patients to control access to specific diagnostic records for a defined period. |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall allow patients to revoke an active consent before its scheduled expiry time. | High | Pass: The selected consent becomes inactive immediately after revocation and the associated records are no longer accessible. Fail: The healthcare provider can still access the records after revocation. | Gives patients control to withdraw previously granted access to their medical history. |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allow patients to view the consent permissions they have granted to clinics, diagnostic labs, and consulting doctors. | High | Pass: The patient can view the healthcare provider, permitted records, consent status, and expiry time. Fail: The system displays incomplete or incorrect consent information. | Enables patients to monitor and manage who has access to their medical history. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall allow authorized healthcare providers to access only the medical records covered by an active patient consent. | High | Pass: The provider can access only the records explicitly permitted by the patient while the consent is active. Fail: The provider can access records outside the granted permission or after consent expiry. | Enforces granular, patient-controlled access to sensitive health records. |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall allow the Clinic Administrator to verify and manage authorized healthcare providers before they are permitted to access patient records. | Medium | Pass: The Clinic Administrator can verify provider details and only verified providers can receive patient-authorized access. Fail: An unverified provider is granted access to patient records. | Helps ensure that patient records are accessible only to legitimate healthcare providers. |
| NFR-001 | Non-Functional | All consent grant, revoke, and record-access events shall be permanently written to an append-only audit trail. | High | Pass: Benchmarking and audit tests confirm that consent and access events are recorded and cannot be modified or deleted through normal system operations. Fail: An event is missing or can be altered. | Provides accountability, traceability, and security for access to sensitive health records. |
| NFR-002 | Non-Functional | The system shall maintain secure and reliable consent enforcement while meeting the required response-time and performance standards under normal and peak usage. | High | Pass: Performance testing confirms that consent checks and access-control operations meet the defined response-time and security standards under simulated peak load. Fail: The system exceeds the defined performance or security limits. | Ensures that patient data remains protected without making the consent management system unreliable or unusably slow. |
